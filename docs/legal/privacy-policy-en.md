# Tiletopia Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 6 November 2026*

This policy explains what data the game **Tiletopia** processes, why, and how you can control it. It is written for players from any country and follows the strictest common standard (the EU GDPR); there are separate notes for Belarus and other CIS countries. Русская версия: https://crownfall90-dot.github.io/TileTopia/privacy.html

## 1. Who is responsible

The data controller is the game's developer, an individual acting under the name **Tiletopia company** (Republic of Belarus). Contact for all requests: `puzzlegrad@gmail.com`. We answer requests within 15 calendar days. The game is for players aged 13 and over. We show no ads, we do not sell data and do not give it to advertisers.

## 2. What data we collect

- **Account.** On first launch an anonymous player ID is created (Firebase Authentication). If you save a profile, we receive your email address and password (the password is stored by Firebase Authentication in protected form; we cannot see it) or your Google sign-in data (email address). The email is used only to sign in and to carry progress between devices.
- **Nickname, colour, figure, frame and status.** You choose them yourself; other players see them in games, in friend lists and in the leaderboard. Do not use your real name in a nickname if you do not want it shown.
- **Game progress.** Level, experience, cups, virtual gold, unlocked items, quests, statistics and match history, friends and invitations. Gold is virtual and is not sold for money.
- **In-game messages.** Moves and short quick phrases in online rooms (kept up to 7 days), and the text of feedback or suggestions you send.
- **Technical data.** App version and platform (Android, web, iOS, Telegram), device model and screen size, crash and frame-rate reports, a push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
- **Usage statistics (only with your consent).** Usage events (Firebase Analytics) and daily counters of opened screens and pressed buttons: numbers only, no texts or names. We use them to understand which features of the game are used. You can give or withdraw consent in Settings → Statistics.
- **What we do not collect:** precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera, payment data, advertising ID.

## 3. Why, and on what basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Running the game: sign-in, progress, friends, online games, leaderboard, update notifications | Performance of the agreement with you (Terms of Use) and your consent when you create a profile |
| Fixing bugs (crash diagnostics) | Our legitimate interest in a stable game |
| Preventing abuse (repeated reward claims, room hijacking) | Our legitimate interest |
| Usage statistics to improve the game | Your consent |
| Answering your requests | Your request |

## 4. Who receives the data and where it goes

- Data is stored and processed in **Google Firebase** services (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics). Google acts as our infrastructure provider and processes data on our behalf. Google's servers are in the United States and other countries, so data is transferred abroad.
- For players in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom the transfer relies on Google's data processing terms and standard contractual clauses. For players in Belarus and other CIS countries, transfers to countries without an adequate level of data protection take place with your consent, which you give when you create a profile. Risks of such a transfer: different legislation and less accessible protection of your rights in the country where the servers are located.
- We do not give data to ad networks and do not sell it. Other players see only what you chose to show: nickname, colour, frame, status, cups.
- Data is sent over a secure connection (HTTPS).
- We may disclose data on a lawful request of public authorities, to the extent the law requires.

## 5. Retention

- Profile and progress: until you delete your account.
- Online room messages: up to 7 days.
- Technical logs and error reports: up to 30 days.
- Usage counters: up to 12 months.
- Database backups: up to 90 days, then erased automatically.
- Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data is kept under Google's rules (up to 14 months) in anonymised form.

## 6. How to delete your account and data

- **In the game:** Settings → Account and data → Delete account and data (or the Delete account button in your profile).
- **Without access to the game:** email `puzzlegrad@gmail.com` with the subject "Account deletion" (give your nickname and, if any, the profile email).
- Instructions page: https://crownfall90-dot.github.io/TileTopia/delete-account.html
- After deletion, backups are erased within 90 days and room messages within 7 days.

## 7. Your rights

You may: find out what data about you is processed and get a copy; correct it (you can change your nickname in Settings); ask for deletion; restrict processing or object to it; receive your data in a portable form; withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect earlier processing); learn who the data was disclosed to. Write to `puzzlegrad@gmail.com`. If you believe your rights are violated, you may complain to the data protection authority of your country: in Belarus, the National Centre for Personal Data Protection; in the EU, the authority of your country.

**Residents of California and other US states:** we do not sell or share data for advertising; your rights of access, correction and deletion work as described above.

## 8. Children

The game is not intended for children under 13 and we knowingly do not collect their data. In countries whose law sets a higher age for consent to data processing (in some EU countries up to 16), a player below that age may use the game only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us data without that, write to us and we will delete it.

## 9. Belarus and other CIS countries

For players in Belarus, processing follows the Law of the Republic of Belarus "On Personal Data Protection" of 7 May 2021 No. 99-Z. By creating a profile you give electronic consent to the processing of the data listed in section 2 and to its transfer abroad as described in section 4. You can withdraw consent by deleting your account or writing to us. The same rules apply to players in other CIS countries; if the law of your country requires data of its citizens to be stored on its territory, tell us and we will consider a separate solution (for example, not saving a profile with an email).

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the game. Material changes that require new consent take effect only after you accept them in the game.

## 11. Contact

`puzzlegrad@gmail.com` or the "Write to the developer" form in the game's settings.
