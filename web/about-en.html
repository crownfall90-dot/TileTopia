Tiletopia: a tile-laying board game

Build cities, roads and monasteries from a shared stack of tiles. Play online with friends, against bots, or pass the phone around. Free, no ads, no purchases.

Play in the browser Download for Android

How to play

  • Take turns drawing a tile from the shared stack and placing it next to the tiles already on the table. Roads, cities and rivers must continue each other.
  • Place a meeple on a city, a road or a monastery: when it is completed you score, even if a rival laid the last tile.
  • Scoring: road — 1 per tile, city — 2 per tile, monastery — 9 when eight tiles surround it.
  • When the stack runs out, the player with the most points wins.

Features

  • 3D board: drag it, pinch to zoom, change the viewing angle.
  • Online: quick matches, rooms for friends by code, leaderboard and cups.
  • Bots of three difficulty levels, also playable offline.
  • Custom rules: stack size, rivers, number of meeples, turn timer.
  • Collections: figures, frames, statuses, emoji and phrases, daily quests and achievements.
  • Your game is saved: close the app and come back to the same move.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and no purchases for real money. Gold in the game is virtual.

Do I need the internet?

Online games need it. Games with bots and on one phone work offline.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the game: Settings → Account and data. More on the deletion page.

Where can I play?

In a browser on phone and computer, on Android (app), and in Telegram.
